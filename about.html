About Our Academy

Excellence in Olympic-Level Shooting Training

Our Mission

Kalyani Shooting Academy is dedicated to providing world-class training in precision shooting sports. We focus on developing discipline, safety awareness, and Olympic-standard techniques in our students.

What We Offer

  • 10m Air Pistol Training: Professional coaching for precision pistol shooting
  • 10m Air Rifle Training: Olympic-standard rifle training programs
  • Safety First: Comprehensive safety protocols and training
  • Competition Preparation: Training for state and national level competitions
  • Modern Equipment: Latest Olympic-grade shooting equipment

Our Approach

We believe in building strong fundamentals, mental discipline, and consistent performance. Our training methodology combines traditional techniques with modern coaching methods to help students achieve their best potential.
